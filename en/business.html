WHAT MAKES HEALTHMAP DIFFERENT

Three things that set us apart.

PRIORITY

Personalized down to the #1 Priority

We don't just provide general health tips. Each person receives a specific first step — their #1 Priority — based on their own blood work, lifestyle data, and a counselor's interpretation.

OPT-IN

The employee chooses to participate

Healthmap is offered as an opportunity, not a mandate. Employees decide whether to use the service — and what to do with the insights they receive.

PRIVACY-FIRST

Personalization without individual data exposure

Individual health data, analysis results, and consultation content are never shared with the employer. The company receives only pre-agreed, anonymized, and aggregated information.

We personalize the priority —
not just the health information.

Most health services provide information or monitoring. Healthmap goes one step further: identifying what matters most for each individual and helping them decide where to start.

LANDSCAPE

Different approaches serve different needs.

Each category below plays a legitimate role. What distinguishes Healthmap is our focus on helping individuals understand themselves and decide what comes first.

HEALTH APPS / PHR

Supporting daily habits

Track activity, nutrition, and sleep on a daily basis. Best for building and maintaining routines through self-monitoring.

PERSONAL DOCTOR / PREVENTIVE CARE

Entrusting health management to a specialist

A dedicated medical professional manages your health proactively. Best for those who want comprehensive, physician-led care.

CORPORATE HEALTH MANAGEMENT

Understanding the organization as a whole

Aggregate health data to manage organizational health risks and compliance. Best for company-wide health governance and reporting.

HEALTHMAP

Understanding yourself and deciding where to start

Integrate multiple data sources, identify the #1 Priority through professional interpretation, and help each person decide their own first step.

OUR APPROACH

Understand. Choose. Start.

Healthmap is not about delegating health decisions to someone else. It's about giving each person the clarity and confidence to take their own first step — informed by data, guided by a professional.

IMPLEMENTATION

How it works for your organization.

01

Offer

The company introduces Healthmap as an available benefit. Employees receive an invitation to participate.

02

Opt In

Each employee decides whether to participate. Enrollment is voluntary and confidential.

03

Insight

Participants receive their Healthmap Insight — including their #1 Priority, Priority Action Plan, and Your Health Map.

04

Privacy

The company receives only pre-agreed, anonymized, aggregated reports. Individual data stays between Healthmap and the participant.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Individual data is never shared with the employer.

Personal health data, analysis results, and consultation content belong to the individual. The employer receives only anonymized, aggregated information within a pre-agreed scope.
