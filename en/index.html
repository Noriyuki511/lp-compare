Healthmap's suggestion: Before adjusting your diet, focus on getting your sleep condition in order first. Limit caffeine to one cup before noon, aim for sleep by 11 PM. Your diet doesn't need to be "perfect" yet.

Healthmap's suggestion: Focus on adjusting your condition through daily lifestyle habits. Start with 15 min of morning sunlight + one fermented food per day.
